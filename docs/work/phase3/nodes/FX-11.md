# FX-11 — zygo doctor icon line shows the ✉ mail and 󰅐 clock glyphs

**Spec:** — (found in the ML-15 plan and review, F5; widened by RT-3 review finding F1). `render::glyphs` (`crates/zygo-hooks/src/statusline/render.rs:255`) returns the shared table built by `table` (render.rs:226, 19 entries; `NERD_TABLE` and `PLAIN_TABLE` at render.rs:250-251). `Glyphs.mail` (render.rs:177) is kept out of that table on purpose (comment at render.rs:176), and RT-3 kept `Glyphs.clock` (render.rs:179, the run-time glyph `󰅐` U+F0150 that RT-3 added to the H§6.5 icon table) out of it the same way (comment at render.rs:178). So the `zygo doctor` icon line (`icon_line`, `crates/zygo/src/cmd/doctor.rs:245-250`), documented as "every Nerd glyph of H§6.5" (H§14 #32), leaves out `✉` and `󰅐`. A user whose font has no `✉` or `󰅐` cannot find that with `zygo doctor`. Input: `Glyphs.mail` (added by ML-15; Nerd `"\u{2709}"` at render.rs:203, plain `"mail"` at render.rs:222), `Glyphs.clock` (added by RT-3; Nerd `"\u{f0150}"` at render.rs:204, plain `"run"` at render.rs:223) and `pub fn glyphs(set: IconSet) -> &'static [(&'static str, &'static str)]` (render.rs:255). `icon_line` (doctor.rs:250) collects every entry of `glyphs(IconSet::Nerd)` and holds no count, so doctor.rs needs no change (it is not in Files, which keeps FX-11 free of WD-17 and MS-18). Depends on RT-3 because both change render.rs and statusline_render.rs.
**Files:** `crates/zygo-hooks/src/statusline/render.rs`, `crates/zygo/tests/usage_doctor.rs`, `crates/zygo-hooks/tests/statusline_render.rs`
