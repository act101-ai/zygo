## Plan

### Design: step 4 under the threshold for json and log (option a)

Two options were weighed (lead's round-2 brief):

- **(a) Run step 4 earlier.** For a `json` or `log` text that only clean or dedup changed and that is over `VIEW_CAP_BYTES - SHAPED_MARKER_ROOM` (7851), run the kind view also under the threshold.
- **(b) Byte cut.** Cut the shaped text of those kinds to 7851 bytes.

**Chosen: (a).** Reasons:

1. O-C9 and the O§8.2 `must_keep` rule. The log view keeps every `ERROR`/`WARN`/`WARNING`/`FATAL`/`CRITICAL`/`panic`/`Traceback` line between the first and the last 20 lines while they fit (`crates/zygo-results/src/shape/kind_view.rs:145-154`). A byte cut keeps only the head: in `capped_log_view_keeps_the_error_line_a_byte_cut_drops` the one `ERROR` line is at line 401, after byte 7851, so (b) drops it and (a) keeps it as `401: …`.
2. It hides less. The log view keeps head and tail and names its cut in the marker step `log errors kept`; (b) ends mid-line with no note and hides the tail, where a log's final status is.
3. It adds no new rule or number. It reuses the kind views of O§7.7 and their cap (`mod.rs:73`), which the spec already ties to O§8.2.

**Scope of the trigger, each limit with its reason:**

- **`diff` is out.** O§8.1 (operator row, 2026-11-24): "a diff under the threshold gets no diff view (O§7.7), so all its `diff --git` lines stay". A diff view under the threshold would drop `diff --git` lines of the sections that do not fit (`kind_view.rs:99-106`). Both (a) and (b) for `diff` would break that operator rule; see Spec gap 1.
- **A runner view is not replaced.** The trigger needs shaper `Clean` or `Dedup` (steps 1-2 only). The runner rows of O§8.2 have no 8 KiB floor, and a runner view keeps every line it does not recognize as noise (O-C9). Over the threshold, step 4 already runs after step 3 (unchanged).
- **No change from steps 1-2 → no trigger.** With shaper `None` the hook gives no view (`trim.rs:203`), so there is no shaped view to cap; `kind_view_not_run_at_the_threshold` (`crates/zygo-results/tests/shape_pipeline.rs:214-218`) stays as it is.
- **Text, tree, jsonl kinds: no cap.** No spec rule caps them (`kind_view.rs:25` gives `None`).
- **A kind view's "no change" under the threshold leaves the shaped text whole.** Step 5 needs the text over the threshold (O§7.3 step 5, `mod.rs:78`). Example: a log with 40 lines or fewer (`kind_view.rs:135-137`). See Spec gap 2.

The spec text for this (O§7.3 step 4, O§7.7 first paragraph and last paragraph, change-log row) is in this plan commit.

### Spec gap (for the lead)

1. **diff under the threshold.** O§8.2 "diff, json, log: view ≤ 8 KiB" and the O§8.1 operator rule (a diff under the threshold gets no diff view; all `diff --git` lines stay; the clean CRLF diff case stays byte-equal except color codes) conflict for a colored diff whose cleaned text is 7852..=16384 bytes. This plan keeps the operator rule, so such a diff keeps a view over 8 KiB (example: `fixtures/shaping/clean/git-diff-color-crlf/view`, 11,361 B, shaper `clean`). Only the operator can change it.
2. **A json or log kind view that gives "no change" under the threshold** (a log of 40 lines or fewer, head plus tail over the cap, a JSON outline that does not fit) leaves the shaped view over 8 KiB, because step 5 needs the text over the threshold. The O§7.7 text now says so. A cap for that case would be a new rule.
3. **Many level lines.** The log view keeps level lines only while they fit (`kind_view.rs:150`); a log with more level lines than fit in 7851 bytes loses some, as it already does over the threshold. Under the threshold that now also applies to a colored log of 7852..=16384 cleaned bytes.

## Outcome

Dropped. The premise is false.

- O§8.2 checks the view of each case against the floor of its own shaper. The node's case (a 20,000-byte colored log at threshold 16384) has shaper `clean`. The `clean` floor is a 5 % saving and has no 8 KiB limit, so the 16,196-byte view breaks no floor.
- Each way to cut such a view under the threshold breaks O-C9 ("No view drops the text that says why something failed") and the O§8.1 `must_keep` rule (each ERROR line of a log). The review of round 1 (0fd61644) showed that the log view under the threshold drops `panicked at`, `Caused by:`, `error: could not compile` and 2 of 150 ERROR lines.
- Round 1 (0fd61644) is discarded. The spec text of the re-plan (65cdeb63: O§7.3 step 4, O§7.7 and its change-log row) is withdrawn; O§7.3 step 4 stays "only over the threshold". The spec change log records the drop (2026-11-26, O§8.2).
