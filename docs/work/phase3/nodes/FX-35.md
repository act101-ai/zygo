# FX-35 — Mail wake refused in the 10 s quiet window; mail-seen and lock bounds

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-22-workspace-mail-design.md` M-G3, M§5, M§7.2, M§7.3, M§12 (found in the Phase 3 final verification stage, 2026-11-28, head b628e1ad). **M-F1 (high).** `crates/zygo-workspace/src/mail/wake.rs:104` returns `Busy` when `!probe.idle(&dir)`; `RealProbe::idle` (`wake.rs:66-77`) applies `drain::idle` (`:75`), whose rule 3 (`crates/zygo-workspace/src/drain.rs:94-96`, `REQUEST_QUIET` = 10 s at `drain.rs:14`) fails for 10 s after the last model request ended. (a) The FX-33 OpenCode turn-end wake (`crates/zygo-hooks/src/workspace.rs:308-327` `wake_after_turn`, `:337-361` `TmuxTurnWake` and `ProbeAt`) runs milliseconds after the last request, so it almost always gets `Busy`; nothing retries, and mail sent while the member was busy is never announced. (b) Any agent: mail sent in the 10 s after a member's turn ended gives the sender's wake (`after_send`, `wake.rs:201`) `Busy`, with no retry, so the member is never woken. This breaks M-G3 ("An idle member is woken"), M§7.3 (the FX-33 text "a wake that is skipped … a later turn end tries again": no later turn end comes) and M§12. The tests `opencode_turn_end_wake_that_is_not_sent_keeps_the_mail_unannounced`, `opencode_turn_end_wakes_once_for_mail_that_came_during_the_turn` and `opencode_turn_end_does_not_wake_without_new_mail` (`crates/zygo-hooks/tests/workspace.rs` near :2178, :2232, :2263) use a fake `TurnWake`. `ProbeAt(now)` applies the injected clock only to the gap: its `idle` calls `RealProbe.idle`, whose `drain::idle` call takes `RealProbe::now`, `SystemTime::now()` (`wake.rs:75`, `:79`). Constraint: no daemon, tmux is the only long-lived process (W-C2, M-G6). **M-F2.** `workspace.rs:489-496`: the `message` event calls `set_mail_seen` before it returns the line; when the plugin's 10 s timer kills the hook, or the hook exits non-zero after the write (`crates/zygo-launch/assets/opencode-plugin.js:8-34` `run`, which gives "" then; `:214-217` the `chat.message` call), the line is dropped but the ids count as announced (M§7.2, M§7.3). **M-F3.** `wake.rs:109` `lock_file(&md.join(LOCK), Duration::MAX)` and the proxy status call of `RealProbe::idle` (`wake.rs:72-74`, `STATUS_BOUND` 2 s) are reached from the OpenCode `stop` hook (`workspace.rs:481-483`); the module contract (`workspace.rs:12-13`) says "No wait here is longer than the 1 s lock bounds of AU56 and W§7.3", and M§5 says "Neither waits more than 2 s".
