### Proposed rules (the operator approves before any code; step 1)

P1 — the mail lifecycle lock (new rule, M§5). Text to add to the M§5 Retention bullet after its last sentence:

> A delivery takes `mail/lock` shared, reads the member records under it, and holds it until its last link into `new/`; it delivers only to a key that has a record in that read, and `zygo mail send` and `mail_send` resolve `to` on that read. A removal of `mail/<key>/` takes `mail/lock` exclusive, reads the member records under it, and removes `mail/<key>/` only when the key has no record. A missing `mail/` needs no lock and is not made. Neither waits more than 2 s: a delivery that cannot take the lock is refused with `zygo: mail is busy; try again`; a removal that cannot take it leaves the maildir, prints one line, and the next removal (`forget-pane`, `up`, `repair`) removes it. `up` removes the maildirs of the records that it dropped after its `phase = up` write or after its undo write, never before, because the undo puts `plan.forget` records back. A read never makes `mail/<key>/`.

Why a lock: the record removal and the maildir removal are two steps, and a sender reads the records before it writes. Only an order between "read records, deliver" and "remove maildir" closes a1-a3. Why a separate lock and not the state `lock` (W§7.3): a send to `all` does up to 32 `fsync`s, and hooks wait for the state `lock` with short bounds (C6). Why shared/exclusive: sends do not wait for each other. Lock order: `mail/lock` is never taken while the state `lock` is held, and the state `lock` is taken under `mail/lock` by nothing (removals read the state without its lock: the file is replaced in one rename, W§7.3), so no lock cycle exists. Rejected: (1) the state `lock` around each delivery (above); (2) make the maildir at record creation and never in a delivery (records that exist today have no maildir until their first message; every test that delivers into a bare `mail/` breaks).

P2 — scope (operator's choice). d1-d4 move to a new node, proposed row: `| FX-<n> | Mail sync deliveries and ws forget take the M§5 mail lock | FX-13, MS-21 | TODO | judgment | — |`. Reasons: d1-d3 are in `zygo-mail-sync`, whose spec decides what happens to an import whose key has no record (S§8.5: the staged file and the ack), and its import tests build no `workspace.json`; d4 is in `ws forget`, which MS-21 (TODO) rewrites. That node consumes `Delivery` (it adds `Delivery::deliver_staged`) from this one. With P2 refused, this plan grows by those four paths before it is built.
